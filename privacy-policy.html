Privacy Policy — Reply Desk Potong Lintang

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Reply Desk Potong Lintang is a personal application operated by Versa Hekmatyar ("we", "the operator") and used only by its operator on the operator's own Threads account.

Data we access

Through the official Threads API, the application accesses:

  • the operator's own Threads user ID and username (threads_basic);
  • public Threads posts returned by keyword search (threads_keyword_search), limited to post text, author username, permalink, and timestamp; and
  • content the operator publishes through the app (threads_content_publish).

How data is used

Keyword search results are used solely to identify public posts in which users ask for product recommendations, so the operator can reply personally. Replies are drafted by the application but are never sent automatically: every reply is reviewed and approved by the operator one by one before it can be sent. Data is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising targeting, and not used to build profiles of Threads users. The application does not like, follow, or repost automatically, and does not scrape Threads.

Storage and retention

All data is stored locally on the operator's own computer. Search results are retained only until the operator deletes them. No Threads data is stored on external servers by this application.

Deletion

The operator can delete all locally stored data at any time from the application's data folder. Any other person whose public post was returned by keyword search may request removal of that record by contacting doktorversa@gmail.com; requests will be honoured within 30 days.
